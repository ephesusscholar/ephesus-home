Who We Are

About Ephesus Home

Founded on faith, compassion, and community support, Ephesus Home of Mary and Joseph Inc. serves as a beacon of love for vulnerable children.

Our Vision

A safe, loving, and nurturing society where every child, regardless of background, grows up with dignity, proper education, and the opportunity to fulfill their God-given potential.

Our Mission

To provide comprehensive residential care, nutritious meals, formal schooling, emotional healing, and spiritual guidance to orphaned, abandoned, and underprivileged children.

Core Pillars

  • Faith & Spiritual Growth
  • Compassionate Protection
  • Academic & Skill Empowerment
  • Community & Family Reintegration

Guided by Dedicated Caregivers & Social Workers

Our multidisciplinary team ensures each child receives personalized care, psychological support, and love.

Comprehensive Care

Our Services & Programs

We provide holistic support designed to transform lives from early childhood through youth development.

Residential Shelter

Safe, secure, and family-like living quarters where children sleep comfortably and feel protected.

Healthcare & Nutrition

Balanced dietary plans cooked daily, regular medical checkups, dental care, and mental wellbeing support.

Formal Education

Enrolling every child in accredited local schools, providing books, uniforms, tutors, and computer access.

Spiritual Guidance

Values formation, Sunday services, character-building activities, and moral empowerment.

Reintegration & Adoption

Working alongside legal authorities to facilitate family reunification or placement with loving adoptive parents.

Recreation & Arts

Music, sports, arts and crafts, and holiday celebrations that build confidence and happy memories.
